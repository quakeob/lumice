Born from Ice & Light

Where frozen architecture meets the aurora

The LUMICE Finland concept imagines a seasonal world of ice and light in Finnish Lapland. Sculptors, architects, and lighting artists would shape crystal-clear ice into immersive spaces that glow from within.

The experience could invite visitors through luminous corridors, frozen halls, and gathering spaces inspired by the northern landscape.
